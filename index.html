About Us

Retro Arcane was born the way the best things usually are. Someone had a vision, and someone else refused to let it stay a dream.

Creative Director Zombri had spent years playing through demos and titles across every genre, finding herself inspired to create worlds of her own. Studio Director Rachel had the technical skills to make it happen. So in 2026, they built a studio.

Retro Arcane is a female-owned, queer-owned independent game studio based in Texas. We make games that feel like something. Cozy and whimsical. Games that make you think. Action-packed and intense. Whatever the concept, we bring the same thing to every project: craft, heart, and a genuine love for the experience of play.

Rounding out the creative team is Game Designer oddE — the connective tissue between art, design, and experience. Whether it's HUD, gameplay feel, or creative direction across both projects, oddE makes sure everything coheres.

We believe you don't have to have a Triple A budget to deliver a Triple A experience. You just have to care enough to get it right.
