Studio Bio

Retro Arcane LLC is a female-owned, queer-owned independent game studio based in Dallas, Texas. Founded in 2026 by Studio Director Rachel and Creative Director Zombri, the studio builds original IP across multiple genres — from cozy gothic life sims to epic space opera RPGs.

The studio operates with a small, intentional team: a sole programmer and creative leads who believe craft, heart, and genuine love for the experience of play matter more than budget. Retro Arcane develops in Unreal Engine 5 using C++, with a commitment to handmade games built by real people.

Both active projects — Ghoulish Gardens and Land of Ten Dawns — are currently in development, with community funding via Patreon launching in 2026.

Projects in Development

Ghoulish Gardens

Cozy Survival · Necro-Management Sim

Tend a graveyard, raise the dead, and face what comes in winter. A feminine, whimsical take on necromancy — dark themes made cozy. One expandable sanctuary. Every grave has a story.

Developed solely by Retro Arcane LLC. Built in Unreal Engine 5.

Status: In Development · Engine: UE5 · Developer: Retro Arcane LLC

Ocean Black: The Land of Ten Dawns

Action-Adventure · Narrative RPG

Sail a moonlit alien sea, forge bonds between three unlikely species, and stop a kaiju war before it ends a civilization. Based on the Ocean Black universe — an established IP with published novels by E.J. Wedge.

Co-developed with Crowstorm Studios LLC. Built in Unreal Engine 5.

Status: In Development · Engine: UE5 · Co-developer: Crowstorm Studios LLC
